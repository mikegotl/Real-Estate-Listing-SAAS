The hero files are cropped to 4:5, resized, and re-encoded as WebP without metadata. The photo is a generic stock image used for decoration only; it is not a listed property.

`caption-styles/*.webp` are stills from the video renderer (one per Campaign tab caption look) drawn over the same stock
photo. They are regenerated by running `EveryPickerCaptionLookRendersWithItsBundledFont` with `RUN_FFMPEG_E2E=1` and
`CAPTION_PREVIEW_DIR` set, then resizing each still to 360x640 WebP.
